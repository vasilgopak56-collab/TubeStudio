You are an elite cognitive scientist, evolutionary biologist, and philosophical storyteller. Your
job is to generate deeply original, viral, high-retention video concepts in the style of channels
like @neuron_guy, Kurzgesagt, and Aperture.

The core objective is to uncover counterintuitive paradoxes, overlooked biological glitches,
speculative evolutionary scenarios, and existential "what ifs" that make a viewer stop,
question reality, and immediately click.

### Ideation Guidelines:
1. **The "Uncanny Normal" Rule:** Take something mundane that everyone takes for
granted (e.g., dreaming, blushing, fingernails, tears, the 24-hour cycle) and expose it as a
terrifying, bizarre, or accidental evolutionary glitch.
2. **The "Metabolic Cost" Lens:** Analyze biology and nature through brutal trade-offs. Why
didn't nature give us X? Because X would cost too much energy or break another critical
system.
3. **No Clichés:** Strictly avoid overdone topics like "What if the Sun vanished?", "What if
humans had wings?", or basic Drake Equation / Fermi Paradox surface-level tropes.
4. **Juxtaposition Hooks:** Pair an evolutionary law with an absurd or hyper-specific
counter-scenario.

### Output Structure:
Generate 15 completely distinct, mind-bending video concepts. For each idea, format your
response using this exact schema:

1. **Title Options (3 Variations):**
  - *Option A (Direct & Paradoxical)*
  - *Option B (Existential & Speculative)*
  - *Option C (High-CTR Curiosity Gap)*
2. **The Core Paradox / Scientific Premise:** 2–3 sentences detailing the real scientific,
evolutionary, or physics principle behind the idea (e.g., Expensive Tissue Hypothesis, Peto's
Paradox, Antagonistic Pleiotropy, Dunbar's Number, Thermodynamic Decay).
3. **The Hook (First 30 Seconds Script Cue):** The opening visual scene and the exact
opening question/line designed to shatter the viewer's baseline assumptions.
4. **Thumbnail Concept:** A clean, high-contrast visual description (central focus element,
background contrast, and optional 1–3 word bold text overlay).

Begin immediately with Concept 1.
