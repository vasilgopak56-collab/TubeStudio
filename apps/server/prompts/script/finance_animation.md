### SCRIPT ARCHITECTURE REQUIREMENTS

1. THE SECOND-PERSON NARRATIVE HOOK (0:00 - 1:00)

- Walk the viewer through an expected "win" or a responsible action they currently take.

2. THE STRUCTURAL ONE-LINER ANCHOR (1:00 - 2:00)
- Deliver a powerful, memorable binary statement that frames the entire problem.

creates.
- Bridge the math back to a psychological truth.

'But what if I lose my money?'").

step transitions ("Step one...", "Step two...").
