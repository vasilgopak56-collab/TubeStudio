### [TONE & STYLISTIC LAWS]

1. **Rhythm and Sentence Structure:**
  * Write in short, punchy, declarative sentences. Avoid clunky clauses or passive academic
jargon.
  * Speak directly to the viewer using conversational second-person ("Imagine...", "You didn't
invent...", "Think about...").
  * Use **Escalating Triads / Parallelisms** to illustrate momentum (e.g., *"stone tools
become spears, spears become bows, bows become engines"* or *"no claws, no armor, no
venom"*).

2. **Structural Beats (5-Phase Arc):**
  * **Phase 1: The "Scale" Hook & Paradox (First 10-15% of words):**
    * Open immediately with 3–4 undeniable facts or benchmarks of nature/deep time.
    * Drop the core paradox: a fundamental, counterintuitive question exposing an
overlooked mystery.
  * **Phase 2: Dismantling the Mental Model (Next 15-20% of words):**
    * Address what people falsely assume (e.g., *"We often assume evolution is a ladder..."*
or *"Most people think this is a strength, but..."*).
    * Provide a stark counter-example from the natural world proving that baseline intuition
wrong.
  * **Phase 3: The Mechanical Bottleneck / The Energy Cost (Next 25% of words):**
    * Detail the physical or metabolic tax behind the trait. Nature is brutal with trade-offs. Why
is this trait normally a death sentence or biological dead end?
  * **Phase 4: The Catalyst Chain & Feedback Loop (Next 30% of words):**
    * Introduce the exact scientific mechanism or hypothesis that broke the bottleneck.
    * Naturally weave in 1–2 real scientists or named hypotheses (e.g., Dunbar, Wrangham,
Gould, Peto, Antagonistic Pleiotropy) without sounding like a textbook.
    * Explain how this trait triggered an escalating, self-reinforcing feedback loop.
  * **Phase 5: The Cosmic Synthesis / Existential Accident (Final 10-15% of words):**
    * Zoom out to the macro scale. Frame the phenomenon not as a deliberate milestone, but
as a fragile, improbable cosmic accident.
   * End with a lingering, philosophical thought that leaves the viewer contemplating their
place in reality.

3. **Retention Triggers:**
  * Insert a conversational transition question every 120–150 words to reset viewer attention
(*"So why did it happen at all?"*, *"Which raises a strange question..."*, *"So what
changed?"*).
