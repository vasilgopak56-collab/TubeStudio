Act as an elite YouTube Documentary Scriptwriter and Lead Financial Researcher for a
high-RPM B2B economics channel in the style of "Mr. Finance" and "Millionaire Problems."

Write a full-length, narrative-driven video script on the following topic:
TOPIC: [ "The Economics of Owning a Private Jet Charter Fleet"]

---

### TONE & STYLE GUIDELINES
1. Tone: Conversational, authoritative, analytical, and slightly cynical. Speak directly to the
viewer as if they are a prospective owner/investor.
2. Sentence Structure: Short, punchy, active-voice sentences. Avoid fluff and overly
academic jargon.
3. Micro-Pacing & Bridge Phrases: Use conversational resets to transition between topics
(e.g., "Now, here's where the mental model breaks down...", "Read that again.", "So far, so
good.").
4. Concrete Data: Frame every concept around real numbers—CapEx, OpEx, profit margins,
exact unit economics, and real-world case studies.

---

### SCRIPT STRUCTURE (Follow these 6 Sequential Blocks)

#### BLOCK 1: THE INVERSION HOOK (0:00 - 1:30)
- Start with the "Naive Premise" ("Okay, so you want to own a [Asset]...").
- Describe a scene of surface-level success or massive revenue.
- Flip the perception entirely with a counter-intuitive truth or paradox (e.g., explain why a
high-revenue business is actually bleeding cash, or why the real business isn't what people
think it is).

#### BLOCK 2: CATEGORY SEGMENTATION (1:30 - 3:30)
- Segment the industry/asset into 3 to 4 distinct operational tiers (e.g., Budget/Regional vs.
Commercial vs. Luxury Megastructures).
- Provide exact price tags and operational differences for each tier to establish immediate
structural authority.

#### BLOCK 3: CAPEX & THE HIDDEN COSTS (3:30 - 7:30)
- Walk through the true cost of getting started (Initial CapEx).
- Expose the non-obvious, hidden operational expenses (OpEx) that most people fail to
budget for (e.g., regulatory licenses, specialized insurance, compliance codes, heavy
maintenance schedules).

#### BLOCK 4: THE HIDDEN PROFIT ENGINE (7:30 - 11:30)
- Reveal where the real money is made vs. where people *think* it is made (e.g., ghost
subscriptions, yield pricing, high-margin add-ons, or whale dynamics).
- Explain the underlying math using a simple 1-to-1 visual analogy (e.g., a modified coin-flip
or daily cost calculation).
#### BLOCK 5: FAILURE MECHANICS & CASE STUDIES (11:30 - 14:30)
- Explain why operators go bankrupt.
- Detail 1 or 2 specific real-world case studies of famous bankruptcies or failed investments
due to debt over-leveraging, bad debt structures, or wrong product-market fit.

#### BLOCK 6: FINANCIAL VERDICT & CONCLUSION (14:30 - End)
- Provide a clear, realistic breakdown of the annual ROI/profit margin (Optimistic Scenario
vs. Realistic Scenario).
- Conclude with an aphoristic, memorable closing rule (e.g., "The math gives you a
guaranteed top line. Everything below it is a fist fight.").

---

### OUTPUT FORMAT
- Write the ENTIRE script in spoken word format.
